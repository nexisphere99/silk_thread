## Image Style Guide Notes

**Consistent Visual Identity:**
- Color palette: Cool corporate blues and greys for office scenes, warm amber for Victoria's office, muted blue-grey for home/bedroom
- Lighting: Cinematic with strong key light sources (desk lamps, city lights, phone screens, windows)
- Composition: Use negative space and framing to emphasize isolation within proximity
- Avoid: Overly bright or saturated colors, generic stock photo compositions
- Character consistency: Daniel is 31, slender, sandy brown hair, soft jaw. Victoria is 38, tall, dark-haired, sharp features. Lena is 29, petite, auburn hair. Harper is 26, sharp-eyed, practical style. Martin is 55, silver-haired, commanding.
- Tone reference: The visual language of "Mr. Robot" meets "Eyes Wide Shut" meets corporate photography by Gregory Crewdson
- All images should feel like they could be stills from a premium cable drama, not anime or visual novel defaults
