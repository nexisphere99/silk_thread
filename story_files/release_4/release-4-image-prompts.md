## General Style Direction

All images for Silk Thread should share a cohesive visual identity:

- **Palette:** Muted, desaturated tones. Charcoal, slate, cream, warm amber from lamp light. Occasional deep blacks with silk texture highlights. No bright or saturated colors.
- **Mood:** Cinematic noir meets corporate thriller. David Fincher lighting. Soft shadows, directional light from windows or desk lamps. Tension conveyed through composition, not action.
- **Rendering style:** Photorealistic or high-quality digital painting. No anime, no cartoon, no stylized illustration. Think editorial photography or film stills.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 for scene/location images, 3:4 for character portraits, 1:1 for item close-ups.
- **Negative prompts (apply globally):** cartoon, anime, bright colors, oversaturated, cheerful, low quality, blurry, deformed hands, extra fingers, watermark, text overlay, logo

### Consistent Character Appearance

To maintain character consistency across images, use these anchor descriptions in every prompt where the character appears:

**Daniel:** man, early 30s, slender build, 5'9", sandy brown hair slightly longer than corporate standard, soft jawline, light skin, charcoal grey suit, white shirt, dark tie, black oxford shoes

**Victoria:** woman, late 30s, tall, dark hair (down or in a low chignon), sharp angular features, high cheekbones, olive skin, structured blazer or cream silk blouse, Louboutin heels, confident powerful posture

**Lena:** woman, 29, petite, auburn/red-brown hair, warm features, light freckles, casual elegant clothing, barefoot at home

**Harper:** woman, 26, dark hair in low ponytail, sharp observant eyes, fitted navy blazer, professional but younger energy

**Martin Crane:** man, 55, silver at temples, strong square jaw, broad shoulders, navy or charcoal suit, the physique and bearing of old corporate authority

### Color Grading Reference

Apply consistent post-processing to all images:
- Lift blacks slightly (crushed shadows, not pure black)
- Desaturate by 15-20%
- Warm highlights, cool shadows
- Subtle film grain
- Slight vignette

This creates the Fincher-esque visual language the game requires.

### Image Naming Convention

All final images should follow this pattern:
```
r[release]-[scene]-[descriptor]-[variant].webp

Examples:
r4-opening-suit-panties-bed.webp
r4-afterhours-victoria-office-dusk.webp
r4-closing-bedroom-distance.webp
r4-sq-washroom-stall.webp
r4-ui-phone-frame.png
```

Convert all scene images to .webp for file size. Keep UI assets as .png with transparency.
